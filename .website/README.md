# Kindness TD

A tower defense game where nothing gets destroyed and nobody gets hurt.

Grumpy hearts are trudging across town. Cheer them up before they reach the far
side, so they can go hang out in the Happy Hangout instead. Ten rounds, five
missed hearts and it's over.

**Controls:** click or tap any tile to open the build menu and pick who to
place. That's the whole game, so it plays fine on a phone.

## Your kindness crew

- **AffirmingWords** (20): says something kind to one grumpy
- **Hugger** (30): holds a grumpy still and cheers them up fast
- **GladRadio** (50): plays to everyone in range at once
- **TherapyDog** (80): sits with up to two grumpies
- **Happy Horn** (free): your hero unicorn, and only one of her ever

Not everyone wants the same help. Headphones tune out AffirmingWords and
GladRadio, a mask means allergic to dogs, thorns mean no hugs thank you.

## Happy Horn

She doesn't stand anywhere. She flies loops around the nearest grumpy, painting
a rainbow that cheers up everyone it touches.

She's free, but she's the only thing your Care Credits can upgrade, and she has
five levels: **white, pink, purple, blue, gold**. Pink and blue make her faster,
purple and gold make her rainbow stronger. Click her to train her.

## Bosses

**Headphone Hank** (round 5) has a huge grumpy heart and no interest in anything
you say, but he's still partial to a dog.

**Negative Neil** (round 10) turns anything he walks near grumpy, so your crew
shuts down around him. Happy Horn is the one thing he cannot sour. Let her
circle him.

## Advanced Mode

Finish all ten rounds to unlock it: every sad meter doubles, Stress Eaters
detour for cookies instead of the exit, and Happy Horn arrives already trained,
her rainbow lasting three times as long.
